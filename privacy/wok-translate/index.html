Privacy Policy 隐私政策

Application: Wok Translate · 中厨助手 (Android)  ·  Effective date: 1 June 2026  ·  生效日期:2026 年 6 月 1 日

Short version. Wok Translate is a fully offline Android app. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not connect to any server. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

简短版本:Wok Translate · 中厨助手 是一款完全离线的 Android 应用。它不收集、不存储、不传输、不共享任何个人数据;不连接任何服务器;不含任何分析、广告或第三方追踪。

1. Who we are / 我们是谁

This application is developed and published by an independent developer (the "Developer"). For privacy-related enquiries, you may contact the Developer at makechange234@gmail.com.

本应用由独立开发者(下文称"开发者")开发并发布。如有隐私相关问题,可通过电子邮件联系开发者:makechange234@gmail.com。

2. Data we collect / 我们收集的数据

None. Wok Translate does not collect any personal data or any other data of any kind from you or your device. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, address).
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI, MAC address).
  • Account or authentication information (there is no account system).
  • Location data (GPS, network, IP-based, or otherwise).
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, or camera content.
  • Usage data, analytics events, crash reports, or diagnostic logs.
  • Cookies, advertising identifiers, or tracking tokens of any kind.

无。Wok Translate 不收集您或您设备上的任何个人数据或其他任何类型的数据。具体而言,本应用不收集:个人身份信息(姓名、邮箱、电话、地址);设备标识(广告 ID、Android ID、IMEI、MAC 地址);账号或认证信息(本应用无账号系统);位置数据(GPS、网络定位、基于 IP 或其他方式);通讯录、日历、照片、文件、麦克风或摄像头内容;使用数据、分析事件、崩溃报告或诊断日志;任何形式的 Cookie、广告标识或追踪令牌。

3. Data stored on your device / 存储在您设备上的数据

The app stores a small amount of information locally on your device only, never transmitted anywhere. This consists solely of your in-app preferences:

  • Language preference (system / Chinese / English).
  • Measurement system preference (NZ / AU / US, which affects cup and tablespoon defaults).
  • Theme preference (system / light / dark).

This information never leaves your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

本应用仅在您的设备本地存储少量信息,从不传输到任何地方。仅包含您的应用内设置:语言偏好(跟随系统 / 中文 / English);计量系统偏好(NZ / AU / US,影响杯和汤匙的默认单位);主题偏好(跟随系统 / 浅色 / 深色)。这些信息从不离开您的设备,卸载应用时会自动删除。

4. Network connections / 网络连接

Wok Translate does not make any network requests. It functions entirely offline. The app does not contact the developer's servers (there are none), any analytics service, any advertising network, or any third party.

Wok Translate 不发起任何网络请求,完全离线运行。本应用不会联系开发者的服务器(没有服务器)、任何分析服务、任何广告网络或任何第三方。

5. Third-party services / 第三方服务

Wok Translate does not integrate any third-party SDK that collects data. The app is distributed through Google Play; the Google Play Store may collect installation and crash information independently as part of its store services. That data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not this policy.

Wok Translate 未集成任何收集数据的第三方 SDK。本应用通过 Google Play 分发,Google Play 商店作为其商店服务的一部分,可能会独立收集安装与崩溃信息。该数据收集受 Google 隐私政策 管辖,不在本政策的范围内。

6. Children's privacy / 儿童隐私

Wok Translate is suitable for general audiences. Because the app collects no data from any user, it collects no data from children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Wok Translate 适合一般用户群体。由于本应用不从任何用户处收集数据,因此也不收集儿童数据。本应用并非针对 13 岁以下儿童。

7. Your rights / 您的权利

Because no personal data is collected, there is no data to access, correct, export, or delete on the Developer's side. You may delete all locally stored preferences at any time by uninstalling the app.

由于本应用不收集任何个人数据,开发者一侧没有任何可供访问、更正、导出或删除的数据。您可随时通过卸载应用删除所有本地存储的偏好设置。

如本隐私政策有更新,新的生效日期将显示在本页面顶部。在法律要求的情况下,实质性变更将通过本应用在 Google Play 上的版本更新说明告知。
